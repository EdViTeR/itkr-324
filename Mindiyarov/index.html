The Hand Tool Approach

At Yarrow, the router and the CNC take a backseat to the chisel, the plane, and the scraper.

We believe that the connection between the luthier's hand and the wood imparts a resonance that machines cannot replicate. Every bevel is felt, every contour is sculpted by touch. This is slow craftsmanship for the modern player.
